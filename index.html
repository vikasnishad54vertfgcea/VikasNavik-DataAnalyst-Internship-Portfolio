Task 4 Highlight

Key Findings — Real Superstore Data

$2.30M

Total Revenue (2014–2017)

Superstore generated $2.3M across 9,994 orders with 51.4% revenue growth. West region led with $725K. Technology had the best profit margin at 17.4%.

-$6.66

Avg Profit on Discounted Orders

Orders with discounts averaged -$6.66 profit vs $66.90 for non-discounted orders. High discounts (>20%) alone generated $135,376 in total losses.

p≈0.000

Hypothesis Test — Reject H₀

Welch T-Test confirmed discounts significantly reduce profit at 99.99% confidence. 95% Confidence Interval: [$64.40, $82.72] entirely above zero.

18.7%

Orders Running at a Loss

Nearly 1 in 5 orders operated at a loss — directly linked to aggressive discounting. Capping discounts at 20% would eliminate most loss-making orders.
